Publish

Save changes never wipes the feed — it keeps every entry and only adds new rows + applies your edits (value, type, labels, confidence). To drop specific entries, tick their checkboxes and hit Purge selected, or use the × on a row; Purge all wipes the entire feed in one click (community intel from pullers included).

Community sources

Pull STIX intel from community sources into the feed. Configure them under otx: and taxii_pullers: in config.yaml. "Pull now" runs a one-shot fetch (works even when a puller is disabled) — new objects appear in the feed and are gated like any community intel.
